Shipping Information

Delivery Options

Lagos Delivery: Free delivery within Lagos. Orders placed before 2pm are delivered same-day. Orders after 2pm deliver next day.

Nationwide Delivery: We deliver to all 36 states in Nigeria. Delivery takes 3-5 business days. Flat rate of ₦2,000.

Express Delivery: Available for Lagos only. Additional ₦1,500. Delivered within 4 hours.

Order Processing

All orders are processed within 24 hours. You will receive a tracking number via WhatsApp once your order is dispatched.

International Shipping

We currently ship to UK, US, and Canada. Delivery takes 7-14 business days. Rates calculated at checkout.

Return Policy

Easy 7-Day Returns

We want you to love your ZAHRA purchase! If you are not completely satisfied, you can return any item within 7 days of delivery.

Return Conditions

  • Items must be unworn, unwashed, and with all tags attached
  • Original packaging must be included
  • Sale items are final sale and cannot be returned
  • Accessories (jewelry, bags) can only be returned if unused

How to Return

1. WhatsApp us at 0813 671 6703 with your order number
2. We will arrange a pickup from your location (Lagos) or provide a return address
3. Once received and inspected, refund is processed within 3-5 business days

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

You can shop directly on our website and add items to cart, or WhatsApp us at 0813 671 6703 to place an order. We accept bank transfer, card payments, and pay on delivery.

How long does delivery take?

Lagos: Same day or next day (free). Nationwide: 3-5 business days (₦2,000 flat rate). Express Lagos: 4 hours (₦1,500 extra).

Can I see the item before paying?

Yes! We offer "Pay on Delivery" for Lagos orders. For nationwide orders, we send detailed photos and videos via WhatsApp before shipping.

What if the item does not fit?

We offer easy 7-day returns. Items must be unworn with tags attached. WhatsApp us to initiate a return and we will arrange pickup.

Do you restock sold out items?

Popular items are restocked weekly. Join our WhatsApp broadcast list to get restock alerts first!

How do I track my order?

Once your order is dispatched, we send you a tracking number and delivery partner details via WhatsApp. You can also WhatsApp us anytime for updates.

Our Story

Born in Lagos, Made for the World

ZAHRA Studio was founded in 2024 with a simple mission: to create premium Nigerian fashion that celebrates the modern African woman. We noticed a gap in the market - Nigerian women wanted trendy, high-quality fashion that understood their bodies, their style, and their hustle.

What started as a small Instagram shop has grown into a beloved brand serving thousands of women across Nigeria and beyond. From Lagos streets to London runways, ZAHRA women are turning heads everywhere.

Our Philosophy

We believe every woman deserves to feel confident, elegant, and unapologetically herself. Our collections are designed to make you feel like the best version of you - whether you are at a board meeting, a birthday party, or a casual brunch.

Made with Love

Every piece is carefully curated and quality-checked by our team in Lagos. We work with skilled local artisans and international suppliers to bring you the best of both worlds - African creativity meets global standards.

Sustainability

Fashion with a Conscience

At ZAHRA Studio, we believe looking good should not cost the earth. We are committed to reducing our environmental impact while delivering the premium fashion you love.

Our Initiatives

  • Recyclable Packaging - All our packaging is 100% recyclable and plastic-free
  • Local Sourcing - We prioritize Nigerian suppliers to reduce carbon footprint
  • Quality Over Quantity - Our pieces are made to last, reducing fast fashion waste
  • Zero Waste Goal - We donate unsold items to local charities instead of discarding

Ethical Production

We partner with factories that provide fair wages and safe working conditions. Every person in our supply chain is treated with dignity and respect.
